ELITE BALL KNOWLEDGE — NIGHTLY CRAWLER SETUP
============================================
Do this once. After it, the player database updates itself every night at
4:00am ET and the live site picks up the changes with no work from you.

You need: a free GitHub account.


PART 1 — MAKE THE REPO (10 min)
-------------------------------
1. Go to github.com and sign in (or sign up — free).
2. Click the + in the top right → New repository.
3. Repository name:  ebk-data
4. Choose PUBLIC.  (Public is required for the free feed to work.)
5. Click Create repository.


PART 2 — UPLOAD THE CRAWLER (5 min)
-----------------------------------
1. On the new repo page click "uploading an existing file".
2. From your Mac, drag in these two files:
      ebk.py                       (the crawler)
      ball-knowledge-MASTER.json   (your current database)
3. Click Commit changes.
4. Now the workflow file. On the repo page click "Add file" → "Create new file".
5. In the filename box type exactly:
      .github/workflows/crawl.yml
   (typing the slashes creates the folders automatically)
6. Paste in the contents of crawl.yml from your download.
7. Click Commit changes.


PART 3 — TURN IT ON AND TEST (5 min)
------------------------------------
1. Click the Actions tab at the top of the repo.
2. If it asks, click "I understand my workflows, go ahead and enable them".
3. Click "Update NBA database" in the left list.
4. Click "Run workflow" → the green "Run workflow" button.
5. Wait. A yellow dot means running, a green check means done.
   The first run takes a while (it verifies every team-season).
6. When it's green, go back to the repo's Code tab. You should now see a file
   called  ball-knowledge-db.json  with a recent timestamp. That's the feed.

If a run fails: click it, click the "crawl" job, and read the red step.
Send me a screenshot and I'll fix it.


PART 4 — CONNECT THE FEED TO THE GAME (2 min)
---------------------------------------------
1. Your feed URL is this, with YOURNAME replaced by your GitHub username:

      https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/YOURNAME/ebk-data@main/ball-knowledge-db.json

2. Open the URL in a browser to confirm it shows a wall of text (that's the data).
3. Go to ebkgame.com/command.html → LIVE DATA FEED.
4. Paste the URL in the box → click SAVE & TEST.
5. The light should turn green and read LIVE with a player count.

That's it. From now on:
  · 4am ET daily — trades, signings, new numbers
  · Sundays — rarity scores refresh too
  · Players get the new data automatically on their next visit

Note: jsDelivr caches for up to 12 hours, so a change committed at 4am reaches
everyone during the day. That's fine for roster news and keeps the feed free
even with heavy traffic.
